I'm excited to announce the publication of our new book, “GPU-Accelerated Computing with Python 3 and CUDA”

Many thanks to my co-author [Niels Cautaerts](https://nielscautaerts.xyz) for the collaboration, 
and to the [Packt](https://www.linkedin.com/company/packt-publishing/) team for supporting us throughout the process.

Our book aims to bridge the gap in low-level CUDA programming for readers who are more comfortable with Python than with C/C++. 
It focuses on writing CUDA kernels directly within the Python ecosystem using Numba CUDA, and also explores deep learning frameworks such as JAX. 
We touch on a range of applications, including image processing, solving differential equations, molecular simulations, and large language models.

It's been a side project over the past 1.5 years, mostly evenings and weekends. 
Hopefully it’s useful for others working on or interested in building more optimized GPU-accelerated solutions in Python.
